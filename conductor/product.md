# Product Guide: The Missing Semester (KR Localization & 2020 Expansion)

## Vision
Expand the Korean localization of "The Missing Semester of Your CS Education" by translating the 2020 curriculum, providing high-quality Korean dubbing for video content, and ensuring the technical concepts are intuitive and accurately conveyed for the Korean-speaking CS community.

## Goals
- **Curriculum Expansion:** Complete the translation of the 2020 lecture notes into Korean.
- **Multimedia Localization:** Download 2020 lecture videos and implement professional-quality Korean dubbing.
- **Linguistic Precision:** Translate global CS terminology in a way that is intuitive for Korean students while avoiding AI hallucinations and preserving technical accuracy.

## Target Audience
- **CS Students:** Korean-speaking undergraduate and graduate students seeking to master the "missing" skills of their CS education.

## Key Features
- **Translated 2020 Lectures:** Full Korean versions of the 2020 course materials.
- **Korean Dubbed Videos:** Audio-localized versions of the original MIT lectures for better accessibility and comprehension.
- **High-Quality Technical Translation:** Manual and verified translation that balances international terminology with local intuitive explanations.

## Constraints
- **Technical Fidelity:** Translation must be accurate to the original MIT content while being culturally and linguistically adapted.
- **Platform Consistency:** Maintain compatibility with the existing Jekyll-based static site structure.
- **License Compliance:** All localized content must adhere to the original CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license.
